### 3. Objectives of the Meeting:
The objective of the meeting was to walk the industry mentor through RAP twin's current progress and dashboard, get feedback on the user interface, and get guidance on two proposed additions: expanded project documentation and a new recommendation-system feature to help users choose a scheduling algorithm.

### 4. Key Discussion Points:
RAP twin's current progress and the live dashboard were demonstrated to the industry mentor.
The mentor reviewed the dashboard's user interface and suggested minor UI/UX refinements for better clarity and polish.
The mentor recommended expanding the project's documentation to cover both usage and technical/API details.
The mentor suggested adding a recommendation-system feature that helps a user choose the most suitable scheduling algorithm/strategy for their workload.
The team and mentor discussed how the recommendation-system feature could be scoped and implemented within the existing pluggable-planner architecture.

### 8. Overall Outcome of the Meeting:
The meeting provided valuable feedback on RAP twin's current state.
